A top-down stealth heist game: slip through a glass skyscraper penthouse at dusk,
dodge patrolling guards and sweeping cameras, crack the safe, grab the art and the
rare watch collection, and escape on the rooftop lift before the alarm maxes out.

## Play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys (mouse click also steers the thief)
- **Interact / pick up loot:** E (or Space near loot, keycards and the elevator)
- **Tools:** Q — EMP pulse (freezes cameras for 6 s, costs 25 score) · F — smoke puff
  (blocks vision for 4 s, costs 15 score)
- **Two floors:** the penthouse and the service level below, linked by the elevator.
  Glass walls look pretty — but guards see straight through them, so plan around it.
- **Win:** grab the penthouse safe (needs the keycard from the bedroom wing) and
  reach the rooftop lift. **Lose:** the alarm meter hits 100 and the grace second runs out.

## Twist

Glass walls let vision cones pass through. Cover is marble, not glass — the lounge
is beautiful and completely exposed. The service floor below is cramped and dark,
but its vents and corridors let you flank the guards upstairs.

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html` in desktop Chrome.
No network requests, no build step. Screenshots are staged with `?scene=cover`
(or `patrol`, `vault`, `getaway`).
